# MyZubster Cyberpunk Story & Comic — How the Project Works

## What this project is

MyZubster Cyberpunk Story & Comic turns **real life, real discoveries and real contributions** into an evolving cyberpunk narrative universe.

The central idea is simple:

```text
REAL LIFE
  -> DISCOVERY / EXPERIENCE / CONTRIBUTION
  -> PHOTO / EVIDENCE / METADATA
  -> MYZUBSTER RECORD
  -> STORY ADAPTATION
  -> CYBERPUNK COMIC
  -> COMMUNITY SERIES
```

The comic is not intended to replace the real evidence. It gives that evidence a narrative layer.

A real observation may become a mission. A real photo may become a flashback, data shard or archive panel. A real contributor may become a cyberpunk character under a public alias. A real project milestone may become part of the canon.

## Two layers: reality and fiction

The project always keeps two layers separate.

### 1. Real-world layer

This can include:

- photographs;
- observations;
- discoveries;
- GitHub contributions;
- bounty submissions;
- public-safe GPS/location data;
- timestamps;
- SHA-256 hashes;
- IPFS CIDs when available;
- verified metadata;
- approved MYZ reward records;
- externally verified settlement evidence when applicable.

These claims must be backed by actual records.

### 2. Cyberpunk story layer

This can include:

- fictional cities and environments;
- holographic interfaces;
- augmented identities;
- robot companions;
- symbolic missions;
- fictional corporations or adversaries;
- dramatized memories;
- narrative interpretations of real events.

Fiction can be imaginative, but it must never be presented as proof of a real platform state, payment or blockchain event.

## Daniel // MyZubster: Origins

The first major storyline follows Daniel Ioni and the formation of the MyZubster world.

The objective is to reconstruct his story progressively from real source material instead of inventing an unsupported biography.

The working structure includes:

1. **Character Bible** — stable identity, visual language, motivations and symbols.
2. **Life Timeline & Canon** — real events and their cyberpunk adaptations.
3. **Series & Episodes** — stories built from the timeline.
4. **Comic Visuals & Covers** — generated and illustrated assets.
5. **Photos & Real-World Evidence** — source material connected to the story.
6. **Metadata & Artifact Index** — machine-readable relationships between story and evidence.
7. **Bounties & Rewards** — contribution programs and verification state.
8. **Publication & Releases** — public editions, galleries and future collections.

## How an episode is created

Every episode should follow this pipeline:

```text
REAL EVENT OR DISCOVERY
        |
        v
SOURCE REVIEW
        |
        +--> photos
        +--> observation records
        +--> GitHub commits/issues
        +--> metadata / hashes / CIDs
        +--> bounty/reward references, if real
        |
        v
CANON ENTRY
        |
        v
SCRIPT / STORYBOARD
        |
        v
CYBERPUNK VISUAL ADAPTATION
        |
        v

        |
        v

```

## Evidence inside the comic

Real evidence can interact directly with the story.

A photograph may be used as:

- a visual reference for a drawn location;
- a memory or flashback;
- an archive card;
- a discovery record;
- a holographic screen inside a panel;
- a source linked from the episode metadata.

Metadata may appear visually as fictionalized HUD elements, while the canonical machine-readable record remains separate.

Example real metadata fields may include:

```text
photoId
observationId
sha256
locationMode
city / region / country
privacyReviewed
exifStripped
cid
bountyId
rewardRecordId
```

Fields are included only when they genuinely exist.

## GPS and privacy

Location is useful for telling the story of real discoveries, but safety comes first.

Public location modes can be:

```text
none
city
approximate
exact-public
```

Exact coordinates must not be published for homes, sensitive infrastructure, protected wildlife, restricted locations or situations where precision creates a privacy or security risk.

The public photographic repository uses sanitized images. EXIF removal does not replace human review of visible faces, plates, documents or other sensitive details.

## Community stories

Daniel's story is only the beginning.

Each participant can create **their own MyZubster cyberpunk identity and series**, based on discoveries and experiences they actually make through the ecosystem.

```text
YOUR LIFE
 -> EXPLORE
 -> DISCOVER
 -> DOCUMENT
 -> VERIFY
 -> TURN THE EXPERIENCE INTO A STORY
 -> BUILD YOUR OWN CYBERPUNK SERIES
```

Participants do not need to copy Daniel. Their city, profession, skills, discoveries and personal journey can create a completely different branch of the shared universe.

Over time, individual stories can intersect and form a larger network narrative.

## Contributors and aliases

Contributors may use public nicknames rather than legal names.

A real contributor should only be represented as having received MYZ when an authoritative reward record supports that claim.

Until a real alias/reward record is available, visual characters must remain clearly fictional, anonymous or concept-only.

## Bounties

Bounties can fund real contributions that later become part of the story universe.

Examples include:

- documenting a discovery;
- creating a comic episode;
- producing a translation;
- building a character guide;
- publishing accessible versions;
- cataloguing photos and metadata;
- connecting evidence to story records.

Canonical lifecycle:

```text
PROPOSED
 -> VALIDATED
 -> APPROVED
 -> FUNDED       # when external funding is required
 -> ACTIVE
 -> SUBMITTED
 -> UNDER_REVIEW
 -> VERIFIED
 -> REWARD_RECORDED
```

A submission, GitHub merge or published comic does not automatically generate a reward.

## MYZ rewards

MYZ is currently an **internal MyZubster reward/accounting ledger**.

`REWARD_RECORDED` means that an approved reward has been recorded inside the platform accounting model. It is not automatically an on-chain transaction.

## XMR or external settlement

A specific bounty may separately define XMR or another external settlement rail.

External settlement follows its own lifecycle:

```text
PENDING
 -> RESERVED / ACCEPTED
 -> SUBMITTED
 -> CONFIRMED
 -> PAID / SETTLED
```

`PAID` requires independent verification appropriate to the settlement rail. Storytelling, artwork, a wallet response, a GitHub issue or a transaction submission alone cannot prove payment.

3. Series & Episodes
4. Comic Visuals & Covers

## What makes this different from a normal comic

The project is designed so that stories can have a traceable relationship with reality.

```text
STORY
  ^
  |
REAL EXPERIENCE
  |
PHOTO / EVIDENCE
  |
HASH + METADATA
  |
OBSERVATION / CONTRIBUTION
  |
REVIEW
  |
OPTIONAL REWARD RECORD
```

The result is a shared cyberpunk universe built from actual lives, discoveries and contributions rather than from fictional lore alone.

## The long-term vision

The goal is to create a global narrative atlas where:

- every contributor can have a character;
- every real discovery can become a story seed;
- every city can develop its own cyberpunk interpretation;
- real evidence stays linked to its source records;
- community series can cross over;
- verified contributions can be recognized through the MyZubster bounty/reward system;
- the archive can grow across languages and countries.

**Discover your world. Preserve the evidence. Transform it into story. Build the MyZubster universe together.**
