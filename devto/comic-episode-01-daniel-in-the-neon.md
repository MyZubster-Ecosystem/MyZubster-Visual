The canonical visual for this episode is published here:

https://github.com/MyZubster-Ecosystem/MyZubster-Visual/blob/main/assets/cyberpunk-series/MyZubster-Cyberpunk-Serie-01-Daniel-nel-Neon.png

## Episode premise

Daniel moves through a city where physical infrastructure and digital systems have become inseparable. Every terminal, sensor, robot, repository and human contribution can become a node in a wider network.

The neon is not only aesthetic. It represents data flowing through the city: discoveries, identities, machine signals, evidence, code and rewards.

Daniel is not watching that network from the outside. He is building inside it.

## The real project behind the fiction

The MyZubster comic is designed to reflect technical ideas that exist in the real project.

The visual story can reference:

- open-source repositories;
- AI and automation;
- robotics and IoT;
- real-world evidence;
- visual asset provenance;
- metadata and SHA-256 verification;
- contributor bounties;
- MYZ reward workflows.

That gives the comic two layers at the same time:

```text
FICTIONAL WORLD
      ↕
TECHNICAL PROJECT
```

The story can evolve creatively, while canonical project artifacts remain publicly inspectable.

## Why build the comic this way?

Most fictional universes are separate from the infrastructure used to build the underlying project.

MyZubster is experimenting with the opposite approach.

```text
REAL PROJECT
   ↓
TECHNICAL EVENT / DISCOVERY
   ↓
VISUAL INTERPRETATION
   ↓
COMIC EPISODE
   ↓
CANONICAL ARTIFACT
   ↓
METADATA + HASH
   ↓
PUBLIC HISTORY
```

A future episode could originate from a robot experiment, a new location, an IoT deployment, an AI agent, a contributor bounty or another real project milestone.

## Episode 01 theme: entering the network

The first chapter establishes the basic idea of the MyZubster universe.

The network is already around us.

Phones, computers, sensors, cameras, APIs, payment systems, repositories and AI models are constantly producing digital state.

The challenge is not simply connecting more things.

The challenge is building systems where those connections remain understandable, inspectable and useful to humans.

That is the world Daniel enters in **Episode 01: Daniel in the Neon**.
