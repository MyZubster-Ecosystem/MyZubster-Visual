# Onion Underground

Status: `CONCEPT_ONLY`
Classification: `FICTION`

## Premise

**Onion Underground** is a fictional privacy-first district inside the MyZubster Comic Universe: a layered digital city of pseudonymous communities, masked avatars, encrypted folklore and conflicting ethical codes.

It is inspired by real-world privacy networks and anonymous online communities, but it is **not** a deployment guide, a real hidden service, or a map to operational cybercrime infrastructure.

## Narrative role

The district represents the tension between privacy and abuse:

- journalists, whistleblowers and dissidents use anonymity to protect themselves;
- researchers and defenders exchange warnings and defensive intelligence;
- opportunistic criminal factions attempt to exploit the same anonymity;
- MyZubster characters must distinguish legitimate privacy from harmful activity without treating anonymity itself as guilt.

## Visual identity

A dense cyberpunk metropolis built in concentric layers:

1. **The Public Rim** — bridges to the open network and visible MyZubster services.
2. **The Relay Bazaar** — pseudonymous markets for ideas, art, tools and reputation; no real illegal marketplace is depicted.
3. **The Veiled Districts** — communities identified by symbols rather than legal names.
4. **The Archive Vaults** — immutable-looking but fictional evidence chambers where story characters debate provenance and trust.
5. **The Black Glass Zone** — a dangerous narrative area associated with scams, malware mythology and hostile actors; details remain fictional and non-operational.

Lighting should use deep shadows, muted neon, layered masks, relay-like architecture and impossible city geometry. No real onion addresses, credentials, exploit commands or active targets should appear in artwork.

## Core characters

- **Metasploit Sentinel** — enters only under explicit defensive scope; observes, records evidence and exits when authorization boundaries are reached.
- **Zorgax** — guide between public MyZubster lore and underground rumors, constantly separating verified fact from fiction.
- **GitHub Chronicler** — tracks what is actually reproducible and refuses anonymous claims without evidence.
- **The Veiled Collective** — fictional decentralized residents with mixed motives rather than a single "black-hat" identity.

## Story conflict

A false rumor spreads through Onion Underground claiming that a critical MyZubster system has been compromised. Multiple anonymous factions repeat the claim, but none provide reproducible evidence.

The episode follows the characters as they:

```text
HEAR THE RUMOR
      ↓
SEPARATE CLAIM FROM EVIDENCE
      ↓
VERIFY AUTHORIZATION
      ↓
REPRODUCE SAFELY
      ↓
DISPROVE / CONFIRM THE CLAIM
      ↓
PUBLISH A SANITIZED EVIDENCE RECORD
```

The dramatic point is that anonymity does not make information automatically false, and popularity does not make it true.

## Safety boundary

This concept may depict fictional hackers, underground communities and hostile intent as narrative elements. It must not include:

- real hidden-service addresses or operational access instructions;
- stolen credentials, malware payloads or exploit chains;
- instructions for evading law enforcement or compromising third parties;
- claims that MyZubster operates or endorses criminal infrastructure;
- claims that Tor/onion services are inherently criminal.

Tor and privacy-preserving technologies have legitimate uses, including journalism, whistleblowing, censorship resistance and personal privacy. The comic should preserve that distinction.

## Canon boundary

`Onion Underground` is currently **FICTION / CONCEPT_ONLY**. Its existence in the comic does not prove that MyZubster operates a Tor service, hidden marketplace, metaverse deployment or anonymous production network.
