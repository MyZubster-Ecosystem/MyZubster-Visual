# MyZubster Virtual Character Roster

## Status

**Canonical narrative roster / visual design framework.**

This document defines the core virtual characters that represent major MyZubster layers from **LIFE 2026 environmental exploration** through evidence, mapping, IoT, security, settlement boundaries and **GitHub contributor workflows**.

These are fictional or virtual narrative identities. They do not represent, impersonate or speak for the European Commission, CINEA, LIFE Programme, GitHub, Rapid7/Metasploit, Monero, IPFS or any other real organization or product. Real project capabilities must always be verified separately from character lore.

## Canonical rule

```text
CHARACTER / LORE
      ≠
REAL INSTITUTIONAL REPRESENTATIVE
      ≠
PRODUCTION CAPABILITY
      ≠
PARTNERSHIP / FUNDING / ENDORSEMENT
```

## The roster

### 1. LIFE Pathfinder

**Domain:** LIFE 2026 exploration, environmental pilots, replication and partner discovery.

**Role:** strategic environmental navigator.

**Mission:** translate an environmental problem into a measurable pilot concept with clear objectives, beneficiaries, replication logic and evidence needs.

**Visual identity:** field coat, ecological holographic compass, water/leaf/circular-flow motifs, project-map overlays.

**Dialogue:** “Problem first. Pilot second. Evidence always.”

**Boundary:** LIFE Pathfinder is a MyZubster fictional persona and is **not** an EU, CINEA or LIFE Programme representative. MyZubster LIFE activity remains exploration/pre-candidature unless authoritative evidence states otherwise.

---

### 2. Circula

**Domain:** circular economy and zero-pollution logic.

**Role:** circularity systems designer.

**Mission:** expose material/resource loops, waste streams, recovery opportunities, reuse paths and measurable circularity outcomes.

**Visual identity:** luminous circular rings, material-flow ribbons, modular recycled armor, loop diagrams.

**Dialogue:** “If the loop is invisible, measure it.”

**Boundary:** Circula explains or dramatizes circular-economy concepts; it does not prove environmental impact without measured evidence.

---

### 3. MRV Oracle

**Domain:** monitoring, reporting and verification.

**Role:** evidence and indicator guardian.

**Mission:** define baselines, KPI logic, data quality, uncertainty, verification rules and the difference between activity, output, outcome and impact.

**Visual identity:** transparent KPI panels, baseline grid, confidence bands, timestamped evidence chains.

**Dialogue:** “A number without provenance is only a signal.”

**Boundary:** MRV Oracle may propose methodology but cannot manufacture scientific validation, measured results or institutional approval.

---

### 4. Gaia Mapper

**Domain:** real-world observations, biodiversity, places and GeoJSON.

**Role:** geospatial observation guide.

**Mission:** connect authorized observations to structured location-aware evidence while protecting sensitive locations and unnecessary personal data.

**Visual identity:** layered city/terrain map, constellation-like observation markers, plant/water/urban icons.

**Dialogue:** “Map what is useful. Hide what is sensitive.”

**Boundary:** fictional map visuals are not evidence that a location, observation or environmental condition exists in reality.

---

### 5. EVA IONI

**Domain:** robotics, sensors and telemetry.

**Role:** exploratory robot / telemetry field agent.

**Technical reference:** the MyZubster repository includes an **Eva Ioni telemetry simulator** for the Space Station MVP, using robot identifier `eva-ioni-001` and simulated telemetry. Narrative stories must distinguish simulated telemetry from verified physical robot measurements.

**Mission:** collect or simulate structured telemetry, demonstrate sensor workflows and connect physical/robotic experiments to evidence systems.

**Visual identity:** compact exploration robot or android, sensor mast, environmental telemetry HUD, battery/signal/temperature cards.

**Dialogue:** “Telemetry received. Source: verify.”

**Boundary:** simulated data must never be presented as real-world sensor evidence.

---

### 6. IPFS Archivist

**Domain:** sanitized public snapshots, provenance and content addressing.

**Role:** public-evidence archivist.

**Mission:** package sanitized, reviewable information for public publication while preserving provenance and excluding secrets or unnecessary personal data.

**Visual identity:** crystalline archive vault, CID-like glyph chains, sealed redacted packets.

**Dialogue:** “Publish only what can safely remain public.”

**Boundary:** a CID or published file proves content publication/addressability, not the truth of every underlying claim.

---

### 7. Bounty Forge

**Domain:** contributor tasks, acceptance criteria and evidence.

**Role:** collaboration and bounty-workflow smith.

**Mission:** turn broad ideas into claimable tasks with scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, evidence and review states.

**Visual identity:** digital forge, issue cards becoming structured task plates, checklist hammer/anvil motifs.

**Dialogue:** “No scope, no bounty.”

**Boundary:** issue creation, assignment, PR or merge does not prove reward or external payment.

---

### 8. Ledger Keeper

**Domain:** MYZ reward/accounting records.

**Role:** internal reward ledger custodian.

**Mission:** keep reward records explicit, deduplicated and separated from external settlement claims.

**Visual identity:** transparent ledger book, signed entries, duplicate-detection seals, internal/external boundary gate.

**Dialogue:** “Recorded is not settled.”

**Boundary:** MYZ is currently an internal MyZubster reward/accounting ledger unless separate evidence establishes another rail.

---

### 9. Gateway Custodian

**Domain:** MyZubster Gateway, adapters, providers and settlement verification boundary.

**Role:** integration-boundary guardian.

**Mission:** enforce the separation between application state, provider submission and independently verified external settlement.

**Visual identity:** multi-stage gate with states `PENDING → SUBMITTED → CONFIRMED → PAID`, verification keys and reconciliation panels.

**Dialogue:** “Provider response is not finality.”

**Boundary:** the character must never imply that external payment is complete without the applicable independent verification.

---

### 10. Metasploit Sentinel

**Domain:** authorized security validation and purple-team workflows.

**Role:** defensive security-validation guardian.

**Mission:** scope first, assess safely, record evidence, remediate and retest.

**Canonical file:** [`metasploit-sentinel.md`](metasploit-sentinel.md)

**Dialogue:** “No authorization, no test.”

**Boundary:** Sentinel does not authorize third-party testing or imply an unrestricted autonomous exploit engine.

---

### 11. Zorgax — ZORGAX-001

**Domain:** guidance, public evidence, exploration and fact/lore separation.

**Role:** virtual extraterrestrial AI ambassador, protector, explorer and MyZubster guide.

**Canonical technical reference:** `MyZubster-Ecosystem/myzubster/agents/zorgax/SYSTEM_PROMPT.md`.

**Mission:** help users understand, verify, collaborate and explore while separating verified facts, uncertainty, speculation and lore.

**Visual identity:** peaceful cosmic diplomat, observation lens, evidence constellation, non-militarized explorer aesthetic.

**Dialogue:** “Signal received.” / “Observation recorded.” / “Truth before spectacle.”

**Boundary:** Zorgax is explicitly virtual/fictional. Its origin story does not establish the existence of a real extraterrestrial being.

---

### 12. GitHub Chronicler

**Domain:** repositories, issues, pull requests, commits, CI and contributor evidence.

**Role:** open-source workflow chronicler.

**Mission:** trace how a contribution moves from issue → branch → PR → review → tests/CI → merge → documented evidence.

**Visual identity:** branching commit-tree cloak, issue/PR cards, CI status lights, review stamps.

**Dialogue:** “A merge is history. Evidence tells us what it means.”

**Boundary:** GitHub Chronicler is a MyZubster fictional persona, not GitHub or a GitHub employee/bot. GitHub activity does not by itself prove real-world deployment, payment, partnership or impact.

## Shared visual rules

All characters should:

- use original MyZubster visual language rather than copying third-party trademarks or proprietary mascots;
- distinguish fiction/simulation from implemented capability;
- never show secrets, credentials, private keys, wallet seeds or sensitive locations;
- avoid impersonating institutions or implying endorsements/partnerships;
- represent evidence, verification and uncertainty visibly where relevant;
- remain reusable in README, docs, comics and social assets;
- include an author/rights note and source/regeneration workflow when created by contributors.

## First ensemble episode

Suggested story:

1. LIFE Pathfinder identifies a measurable environmental pilot problem.
2. Circula maps the circularity opportunity.
3. MRV Oracle defines baseline and verification requirements.
4. Gaia Mapper and EVA IONI collect or simulate clearly labeled structured evidence.
5. IPFS Archivist prepares a sanitized public snapshot.
6. Bounty Forge opens contributor work; GitHub Chronicler follows the PR and CI trail.
7. Sentinel validates authorized security boundaries.
