# Email relationship bridge — MyZubster canon

## Purpose

This document connects **relationship roles evidenced by project email correspondence** to the MyZubster narrative/visual universe without turning real people into fictional characters, publishing private email addresses, or implying partnerships that have not been formally established.

Email correspondence is treated as **private-source project evidence**. It may establish that a conversation, introduction, expression of interest, contributor action, or meeting discussion occurred. It does **not** by itself establish a signed partnership, funded LIFE project, institutional endorsement, payment, employment, or other formal relationship.

## Privacy rule

Public canon should use a real person's name only when that identity is already public and necessary to understand a documented project interaction. Never publish private email addresses, message IDs, attachments, signatures, phone numbers, or unrelated personal information from correspondence.

Where possible, visual stories should represent external contacts through **role archetypes** rather than likenesses or impersonations.

### Deployment Automation Node

Evidence class: automated service notifications.

Observed context: GitHub-linked deployment/status notifications from external hosting automation.

MyZubster connection:

`GitHub Chronicler → Gateway Custodian → deployment/status evidence`

Narrative use: represent these as generic **Deployment Drones** or **CI Messengers**, not as human characters.

Boundary: a deploy-preview notification or CI message proves only the reported automation event. It does not prove that production is healthy, fully deployed or independently verified.

## Visual-story rule

Real external people are **not automatically MyZubster characters**. The default transformation is:

```text
PRIVATE / PROFESSIONAL CORRESPONDENT
        ↓
EVIDENCED PROJECT ROLE
        ↓
PRIVACY-SAFE ARCHETYPE
        ↓
MYZUBSTER STORY NODE
```

A named or likeness-based character should require explicit permission or an already-public, clearly appropriate identity context.

## Evidence states

Use these labels in future canon and metadata:

- `correspondence_observed` — an email exchange exists;
- `discussion_open` — correspondence indicates an ongoing discussion;
- `public_contributor_activity` — public GitHub activity corroborates the interaction;
- `proposed_collaboration` — collaboration has been proposed but not established;
- `formal_relationship_verified` — use only when separate authoritative evidence proves the relationship.

Never upgrade the first four states to `formal_relationship_verified` solely from email correspondence.

## Canonical rule

**Emails connect people to the MyZubster evidence graph; they do not automatically convert people into partners, characters, endorsements or verified institutional relationships.**
